# Upstream attribution

astralix Userbot is a modified fork of [ZetGoHack/Heroku](https://github.com/ZetGoHack/Heroku), dev archive, itself derived from Hikka and Friendly-Telegram. Original copyright headers, including Codrago and Dan Gazizullin, and the GNU AGPL-3.0 license are retained. Rebranding does not change their authorship or licensing.

astralix-tl is a modified distribution of HerokuTL, based on Telethon. Sources were obtained from [sepiol026-wq/herokutl-sources](https://github.com/sepiol026-wq/herokutl-sources) at commit `2537d6f5bff7b86f99b074ce2db967f788ede093`. The library is installed from its [separate repository](https://github.com/lowsense-dev/astralix-tl), where its MIT license and original copyright notices are retained. The import namespace is now `astralixtl`; the distribution is `astralix-tl`, version `1.0.0`.

Original source headers are retained verbatim and followed by the LowSense / astralix header. Upstream repository URLs identify provenance, not astralix community channels. Historical changelogs may also mention upstream names. Dependencies retain their respective licenses.

Telethon v1 remains MIT on [Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/Lonami/Telethon/src/branch/v1/LICENSE). Its original LonamiWebs copyright line, omitted by the intermediate HerokuTL archive, is restored in the [astralix-tl license](https://github.com/lowsense-dev/astralix-tl/blob/main/LICENSE.md).
